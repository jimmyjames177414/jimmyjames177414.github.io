01 About

I'm James Robert Miller. Online I go by jimmyjames177414, on GitHub and everywhere else.

I started building software at Purdue, as lead developer on a Department of Defense grant-funded disaster-response system and as an application developer at No Magic, then spent three years on Saggezza's UX team before joining Accenture in 2018.

Today I'm an AI architect on Amethyst, Accenture's internal AI assistant, and one of the developers who originally built it at a 2019 hackathon. I own its orchestration layer across Amazon Bedrock, Azure OpenAI and Google Vertex AI. Lately I spend as much time on how teams build with AI as on what they build: context is an asset, and an AI is only as useful as what your team has written down.

On my own time I build tools that measure what AI coding agents actually do: what context they needed, what they changed, what they broke, and what they quietly got wrong.

Every project starts with a written prior-art search before any code, including a list of claims the project is not allowed to make. Outside all that: Arduino, Raspberry Pi, and taking things apart to understand them.
